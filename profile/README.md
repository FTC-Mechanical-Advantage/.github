# FTC Team 24799 — Mechanical Advantage

We are FTC Team 24799, Mechanical Advantage, a student-led robotics team based in Flower Mound, Texas.

We design, build, program, and compete with robots for the FIRST Tech Challenge. Our team focuses on thoughtful engineering, reliable systems, and constant iteration—using each event as a chance to improve both our robot and the way we work together.

## Recognition

- FIRST Tech Challenge World Championship - Division Inspire Award — 2nd Place
- Texas UIL 6A State Championship - Winning Alliance
- Texas State Championship Division - Inspire Award — 3rd Place
- Texas State Championship Division - Finalist Alliance
- North Texas Regional - Design Award
- North Texas X-League Tournament - Inspire Award - 1st Place
- North Texas X-League Tournament - Winning Alliance Captain
- North Texas McKinney Qualifier - Winning Alliance
- North Texas McKinney Qualifier - Reach Award

## What We Value

- Building robots that work consistently under pressure
- Learning through testing, failure, and iteration
- Sharing engineering knowledge with our community
- Creating a team culture where everyone can contribute and grow

This team only competed in the 2025-2026 FTC Decode Season.
